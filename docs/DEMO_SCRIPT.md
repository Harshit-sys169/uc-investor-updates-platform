# Demo Script

## 60-second version

1. Open the landing page.
2. Jump to the showcase page.
3. Open the dashboard and explain the tenant-aware shell.
4. Open investors and show import + dedupe.
5. Open updates and show the block editor.
6. Open AI and show drafting support.
7. Open analytics, intelligence, enterprise, and observability.
8. End on the deployment and testing story.

## 3-minute version

### Problem
Founders lose time turning raw company updates into investor-friendly communication.

### Solution
This platform stores investor relationships, drafts updates, sends messages, tracks engagement, and surfaces intelligence.

### Why it is notable
The repo covers product, engineering, operations, and presentation. That makes it appropriate for internship and junior engineering review.
